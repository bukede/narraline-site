Narraline Loop privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Narraline Loop is an internal tool operated by Narraline Limited (Hong Kong). It is used only with Narraline's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API, the tool uploads Narraline's own videos, captions and thumbnails, updates their titles, and reads aggregate analytics for the channel (views, watch time, retention, subscribers, impressions and click-through rate).

How the data is used and stored

Analytics are stored on Narraline's own computers and used only to decide which topics, titles and thumbnails to make next. OAuth tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Viewers' personal data

The tool reads only aggregate channel reports. It does not collect personal information about viewers.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Through the email listed on the Narraline YouTube channel's About page.
